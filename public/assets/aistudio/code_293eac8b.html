Empirical Validation
Tobii Pro Eye Tracking Heatmap Analysis
N = Lab Cohort
Key Biometric Insights:
  • • Strongest fixation: Headline “Timeless Classics. Now Streaming.” captured the fastest gaze lock across all respondents.
  • • Schema violation: Modern AirPods on Mona Lisa and Girl with Pearl Earring caused deliberate repeat gaze fixation and extended dwell time.
  • • Brand saliency: Spotify green circular insignia achieved rapid time-to-first-fixation without cannibalizing visual narrative.
  • • Receipt engagement: Heatmaps confirmed eye sweeps down specific song titles and total minutes listened.
Methodology: Tobii Screen-based Eye Tracker

Internship Project · Proposed 2025 · BIMTECH

Rozana Ki Baarat — Shaadi-Style Guerrilla Activation

A high-energy, shaadi-style street activation conceived for Rozana's Tier 3 and 4 store launches to drive viral footfall through FOMO, hyperlocal community energy, and cultural resonance.

Creative Mechanics:

Rozana marries “Saste Daam” in an authentic celebratory shaadi procession through the town!

Rozana = Dulha (Groom)
Saste Daam = Dulhan (Bride)
Customers = Baraatis
Store = Shaadi ka Mandap

Featured “The Wall of Fame Auto” adorned with local customer portraits, wedding-invite style promotional flyers, naach-gaana, and free wedding sweets.

Note: Proposed internally during internship at Rozana. A near-identical shaadi-themed concept was later executed at scale by quick-commerce unicorn Zepto.

Internship Project · Proposed 2025 · BIMTECH

Rozana Ki Baarat — Guerrilla Campaign Activation

“Shaadi-Style Street Activation for Tier 3 & 4 Bharat”

Conceived during an internship at Rozana to drive explosive footfall and hyperlocal engagement for new retail store inaugurations in Tier 3 and 4 markets across Uttar Pradesh.

Core Strategic Insight:

Traditional Indian weddings are zero-friction cultural unifiers in small towns. Framing deep grocery discounts as a community marriage eliminated corporate cynicism and created unstoppable organic word-of-mouth.

Case Study Visual Evidence & Slides:

Presentation Slide: Concept 'The Wall of Fame Auto' & Shaadi Procession

Key Deliverables & Strategic Output:
  • •Procession Architecture: Rozana (Dulha) Marries Saste Daam (Dulhan)
  • •Hyperlocal 'Wall of Fame Auto' decorated like wedding rath with customer photos
  • •Wedding-invitation styled promotional discount flyers delivered to homes
  • •Naach-gaana, dholak procession, and complimentary wedding sweets at the store mandap
