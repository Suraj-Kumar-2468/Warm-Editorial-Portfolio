## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, tactile, and warmly sophisticated aesthetic crafted specifically for a high-impact Social Media & Content Marketing Specialist. It merges the deliberate, literary pacing of independent magazines with the kinetic clarity required for digital marketing, growth campaigns, and creator analytics.

### Emotional Profile
- **Warm Authority:** Communicates strategic command without clinical austerity. The interface feels organic, inviting, and human-crafted.
- **Editorial Poise:** Evokes the tactile sensation of high-grade paper stock, refined typography, and thoughtful curation.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Punchy metrics and vivid campaign highlights assert high conversion performance alongside aesthetic discipline.

### Design Movement & Language
The system adopts an **Editorial Contemporary Tactile** framework:
- Uncoated, natural-light backdrop values reminiscent of alabaster and raw linen.
- Ultra-soft micro-surfacing with delicate borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Subtle frosted glassmorphism reserved exclusively for persistent utility components (such as floating navigation bars and context menus).
- Curated asymmetric grid structures balanced by pill-shaped interactive anchors.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in sun-baked earth, natural linen, and deep roasted pigment, eschewing synthetic tech blues or pure digital blacks.

### Roles & Harmonies
- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta):** Represents vitality, conversion, and creative focus. Used for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and high-priority stat highlights.
- **Secondary (`#606C5A` - Sage Olive):** Grounded, botanical counterpoint. Applied to secondary chips, growth statistics, client deliverables, and subtle badge backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#E07A5F` - Warm Coral):** A luminous accent for hover highlights, notifications, dynamic gradients, and engagement-rate counters.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1F1E1D` - Rich Espresso):** High-density dark tint for typographic hierarchy and sharp iconography, delivering high readability without the harshness of `#000000`.
- **Canvas & Surface System:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Alabaster / Linen)
  - Surface Tinted: `#F3EFEA` (Card surfaces, nested sections)
  - Surface Elevated: `#FFFFFF` (Floating cards, modals, popovers)
  - Ghost Border: `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08)` (Subtle perimeter structural lines)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast literary charm of **Playfair Display** with the geometric precision and open counters of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Principles
- **Headlines:** Set in Playfair Display with deliberate optical tracking to reflect an art-directed print spread. Large italic variations are encouraged for highlighted impact words within project titles.
- **Body & Data:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures crisp legibility across dense campaign metrics, strategies, case study narratives, and body copy.
- **Numerics & Growth KPIs:** Metrics utilize `metric-display` (Playfair Display) for the primary value, paired with `label-md` uppercase descriptors to highlight scale and impact.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a flexible 12-column grid across desktop and tablet viewports, collapsing into a 4-column system for mobile.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max inner content container of 1280px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Spatial Flow
- Generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` to 6rem) separates key portfolio milestones (e.g., Campaign Case Studies, Growth Statistics, Content Gallery, About).
- Interior component layout stays tight (`space-xs` to `space-md`), balancing breathing space with density in data badges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile warmth and subtle environmental layering over intense drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
1. **Linen Floor (Level 0):** `#FAF8F5`. Flat background layer providing a soft paper-like base.
2. **Resting Cards (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EFEA` with a 1px perimeter border rendered in `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.06)` and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive & Hovering (Level 2):** Elevated cards on hover transform with a translate-y of `-3px` and an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.04)`.
4. **Frosted Navigation (Level 3):** Semi-translucent glass bar utilizing `background: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and a lower border of `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates intentional curve radii, pairing rounded containers with organic pill elements.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Standard (`roundedness: 2` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, inline metric modules, and content preview thumbnails.
- **Prominent (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px to `rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Portfolio case study cards, featured editorial containers, and testimonials.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter buttons, status badges, social media platform chips, and primary CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled terracotta (`#C85A32`) with clean white (`#FFFFFF`) text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state lifts with a subtle warm glow (`#E07A5F`).
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, rich espresso border (`rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.2)`), espresso text (`#1F1E1D`). On hover, transitions to sage olive background (`#606C5A`) with white text.
- **Tertiary / Text:** No border, bold editorial underline with a `2px` offset in terracotta.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Category Filter Pills:** Padded `0.375rem 1rem`. Inactive state sits on `#F3EFEA` with `#1F1E1D` text. Active state utilizes rich espresso (`#1F1E1D`) with `#FAF8F5` text.
- **Platform Tags:** Subtle tinted pill backgrounds with low-opacity icon indicators (e.g., TikTok, Instagram, Substack, LinkedIn).

### Social Metrics Badges
- Compact cardlets with `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-lg` (16px), bordered in `rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.06)`.
- Metric numbers displayed in Playfair Display (`metric-display` scale), accented by tiny sage olive (`#606C5A`) or warm terracotta (`#C85A32`) upward trend vectors (e.g., `+184% MoM`).

### Cards (Case Studies & Content Previews)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with `rounded-xl` (24px) corners, padded with `1.75rem`.
- **Media Wrapper:** Embedded image/reel ratio containers with `rounded-lg` (16px) corners and `overflow: hidden`.
- **Tagging:** Stacks 2–3 pill chips at the top edge for easy campaign categorization.

### Form Inputs
- Understated input panels with `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border (`rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.15)`), `0.75rem 1rem` padding, and `0.5rem` radius.
- Active focus state applies a crisp `1.5px` border in `#C85A32` without harsh browser focus rings.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Radios
- Rounded indicators (`4px` for checkboxes, full circle for radios). Active state fills in terracotta (`#C85A32`) with white check icon.

### Floating Editorial Header
- Compact height (64px), centered content container, sticky top positioning with frosted glass aesthetic (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` + `blur(12px)`). Padded with pill navigation items.
